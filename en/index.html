FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The short version on price, timelines and payment. Can't find your answer? Email me.

How much does it cost and what affects the price?

Starting prices are in the services table. The final price depends on the scope. I give you the exact amount before work begins, and it does not change without your agreement.

How does payment work?

Usually a 50% deposit and the rest after delivery. For small tasks you can pay in full on completion. We agree on the payment method in advance.

How long does it take?

A website takes 2–5 days, a bot 3–7 days, small tasks from 1 day. If it is urgent, write to me and we will discuss it.

What do you need from me?

A description of the task and, if you have them, examples, texts and photos. If something is missing, I will help you put it together.

How many revisions are included?

Small edits during the work and for a week after delivery are free. Major changes after delivery are estimated separately.

Do I pay for hosting and a domain?

Yes, but the amounts are small: hosting and support on my server from 400 ₽ ($5) a month (HTTPS, backups, small edits), and a domain for about 300 ₽ ($4) a year, registered in your name. See “What a website costs” above.

Who owns the files and code?

You do. After payment I hand over all source files and access.

Do you use AI?

Yes, it speeds up the work. But I check the result myself and take responsibility for it.
