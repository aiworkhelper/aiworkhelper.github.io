How to print

  • Lay it on its side, flat face down on the bed: no supports needed.
  • 0.2 mm layers, 3–4 perimeters, 20–30% infill.
  • The mesh was checked in code: it is watertight and ready for a slicer.

Profile drawing

The stand is 70 mm wide. All dimensions can be changed at the top of stand.py, then rebuild the model with python stand.py.
